# Priority Feature Backlog - Executive Summary

## 🚨 CRITICAL (Must Have for Market Entry)

### 1. Segmentation Engine ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Can't do personalized marketing without it. Every competitor has this.
**Impact:** Enables 80% of real-world use cases
**Effort:** 3-4 weeks
**ROI:** 10x (unlocks enterprise customers)

**What it enables:**
- Target "High-value customers in California"
- Send different content to different industries
- Create lookalike audiences
- A/B test by segment

---

### 2. Lead Scoring ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** B2B marketing requires knowing who's sales-ready
**Impact:** Increases conversions by 20-40%
**Effort:** 2-3 weeks
**ROI:** 8x (B2B is 60% of market)

**What it enables:**
- Auto-qualify leads for sales
- Prioritize hot prospects
- Trigger "sales handoff" automation
- Measure engagement quality

---

### 3. A/B Testing ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Standard feature. Can't optimize without it.
**Impact:** Improves performance by 15-25%
**Effort:** 3-4 weeks
**ROI:** 7x (proves marketing ROI)

**What it enables:**
- Test subject lines
- Test send times
- Test content variations
- Auto-select winners

---

### 4. Goal Tracking ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Must prove automation ROI
**Impact:** Justifies marketing spend
**Effort:** 2 weeks
**ROI:** 6x (required for enterprise)

**What it enables:**
- Track conversions per automation
- Calculate revenue attribution
- Measure campaign effectiveness
- Report to executives

---

### 5. Dynamic Content ⭐⭐⭐⭐
