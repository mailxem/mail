Hiring teams: it’s time to check IDs at the door

AI and hiring were supposed to be a good match: faster screening, smoother processes, better signals.

But lately they’ve brought an uninvited guest: fraud. Our 2026 Hiring Report found that most recruiters have spotted, or suspect, candidates misrepresenting themselves in the past year.

This edition looks at that trust gap, and how to close it without slowing hiring down.

The recruiter’s desk: four ways to manage risk

Tip 1: treat AI as input, not authority. Use it like a smart colleague’s opinion, not the final word.

Tip 2: replace black boxes with glass boxes. Teams should be able to see how a tool reached its answer.

Tip 3: check identity from start to finish. Verification shouldn’t stop at the application.

Tip 4: ask for explanations. Audit trails and clear reasoning keep everyone honest.
