Hi there,

This month we’re celebrating the many different ways kids think and move through the world.

Because belonging isn’t about changing who you are. It’s about being known, respected and welcomed exactly as you are.

Here are a few books, shows and resources to help your family talk about it with more understanding and more joy.

Books and shows to share with your kids

Finn’s Big Ideas (TV series)
A boy with a huge imagination uses it to make sense of the world. Gentle, funny and full of big feelings.
Many Ways to Say Hello
A picture book about a child who doesn’t speak much but has plenty to say, through gestures, drawings and play.
My Stripes
A child worries that one thing about him is all anyone sees, and learns it’s only one part of who he is.
