A guided session at a florist’s home studio, in pictures and her own words.
F E R N   &   S T E M

Last week, we spent an afternoon at florist Lena Moss’s home studio for a guided flower-arranging class.

Lena has been a customer since our first year. She runs a small studio, teaches evening classes, and arranges flowers for a handful of local restaurants.

“I started arranging flowers as a break from a desk job. It was only meant to be for fun. Ten years later, it’s the job.”

“Arranging isn’t about filling the vase. It’s about knowing which stem to leave out.”

“I keep my classes small. I want everyone to leave with something that looks like theirs, not like mine.”
THE CLASS
The afternoon started with a short breathing exercise and a playlist Lena made for the day. Each table had a vase, a pair of snips, some wire and a notebook for sketching.

“It doesn’t have to be full. It doesn’t have to be symmetrical. It just has to feel right to you.”

“Start with the vase. Then build up, a few stems at a time, and stop sooner than you think.”

Afterwards, everyone washed up with our citrus hand wash and finished with the daily hand cream.
