ONE ROOM · THE LIVING ROOM

A dim living room, fixed in a weekend.

The living room faces north and always felt gloomy by four o’clock. We painted the walls a warm white, moved the sofa off the window wall, and added two lamps at eye level instead of relying on the ceiling light.
WHAT WE USED
3 tins of paint
2 floor lamps
1 rug, second-hand
6 picture frames
WHAT IT TOOK
Two days, one friend with a van, and a lot of masking tape.
