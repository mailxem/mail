Your Tern journey starts now

Welcome to Tern, your coding companion. Let’s get you to your first commit in the next five minutes.
Step 1: Let Tern learn your project
Open your project folder and run tern. Then type:

> /learn

Tern writes a short notes file about how your project builds and tests. Then ask it to commit that file.
Step 2: Make your first change
Ask for something small: a fix, a test, a tidy-up.

> Add a feedback button and a test for it

Give feedback as it works, or approve edits as you go.
Step 3: Commit it
When you’re happy, tell Tern:

> Commit and push this change
