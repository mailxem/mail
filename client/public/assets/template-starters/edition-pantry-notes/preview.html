Crispy butter beans with greens and lemon

Serves 2 · 15 minutes · One pan
YOU’LL NEED
1 tin butter beans, drained
3 tbsp olive oil
2 garlic cloves, sliced
1 bunch chard or kale
1 lemon
Chilli flakes, salt
METHOD
1. Pat the beans dry. Fry in the oil over a high heat, without stirring, until the bottoms crisp.

2. Add the garlic and chilli for a minute, then the greens and a splash of water.

3. When the greens wilt, take it off the heat. Zest and squeeze in the lemon. Salt to taste.
Tip: dry beans crisp; wet beans steam. Give them five minutes on a tea towel first.
