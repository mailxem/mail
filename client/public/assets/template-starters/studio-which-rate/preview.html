🎓 Loan basics: Types of interest rate

Which rate is right for you?

When you take out a home loan, you’ll usually choose between a fixed rate and a variable one. Here’s the difference:
Fixed rate
Steady and predictable

• Your rate stays the same for the whole term, so your payments do too.
Variable rate
Lower to start

• Usually cheaper for the first few years, then it follows the market up or down.

How to choose

A long fixed term
If you plan to stay put for years and want a steady payment, this is the one.

A variable rate
If you might move or switch within a few years, the lower starting rate could save you money.
