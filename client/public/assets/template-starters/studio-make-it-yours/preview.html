Customers often tell us the best thing about Blockwise is how far they can change it. Here are a few ways to make the editor look and feel like part of your product:
Level: beginner

Match the editor to your brand

People shouldn’t feel they’ve left your product to use it.

Use themes for your colours and fonts, or custom CSS for full control over the look.
Level: intermediate

Choose which features to switch on (or off)

Want mobile editing? Don’t want people pasting raw HTML? Tick a box in the developer console.

Language, sidebar position and much more live in your config file.
Level: advanced

Build your own content blocks

Custom blocks let people pull in content from your own product: a catalogue item, an event ticket, a booking link.
