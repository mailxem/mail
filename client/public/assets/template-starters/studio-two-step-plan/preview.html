How to buy in this market

Understanding how refinancing works, even if you’re just looking, can help you buy with more confidence. Here’s a two-step plan:
1
Buy when you’re ready
2
Refinance to lower your payments if rates fall
Buying now means you start building equity, even if the numbers aren’t perfect. If rates feel high, your loan adviser can show you how refinancing later might lower your payments.

Your refinancing checklist

When refinancing makes sense:
•  Rates drop enough to meaningfully lower your payment
•  You want to change your term, say from 30 to 15 years
•  The long-term savings outweigh the fees
