Support teams don’t get harder in one big way as they grow. They get harder in four particular ways, and most teams are living with at least one of them without a name for it.

Join us on Thursday 27 August at 11am Pacific to go through all four. You’ll probably recognise one:

The Lost Thread. By the time a question reaches someone who can answer it, the background is gone.

The Bottleneck. Everything goes through one person, and they can’t take a holiday.

The Reinventor. The same problems get solved from scratch every quarter.

The Stretched Lead. Volume is up, the team isn’t, and quality is fraying.

You’ll leave knowing which one is yours, and what a team built to avoid it looks like.
