The newest class of Obama Leaders is here!
President Obama champions the fundamental belief that change doesn’t come from the top down—it belongs to the next generation of leaders. Through the Obama Leaders Program, an eight-month leadership development initiative, The Obama Foundation equips changemakers ages 25–44 across the government, business, and civil society sectors with values-based leadership skills and a network of support.
Now, 155 leaders from 64 nations and territories will learn from each other, embrace new perspectives, and gain the tools they need to turn hope into action.
Check out their bios and get to know the 2026–2027 Obama Leaders.
Meet the Leaders
Among the 2026–2027 Obama Leaders are:
  • Members of parliament in Germany, Greece, and Türkiye
  • A city councillor from Niger
  • A municipal court judge in the Philippines
  • A social inclusion leader advocating for children’s rights in Albania
  • Organizers registering first-time voters in the United States
But the power of the Leaders program isn’t in each individual Leader, it’s in the power of bringing them together.
And the collaboration won’t end in 2027. Upon completion of the Leaders program, they’ll join the Obama Leadership Network, a growing global alumni community of nearly 1,900 active changemakers across over 160 countries.
