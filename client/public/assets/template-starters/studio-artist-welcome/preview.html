Welcome!

I’m so glad you’re here.

I’m Rosa, an abstract painter making original work about home, balance and belonging. I use natural pigments, handmade paints and archival materials, so each piece brings a little warmth to a room.

Whether you’re a collector or just starting out, I hope my work gives you a moment to pause. From small framed pieces to large paintings, each one is made slowly and on purpose.

Thank you again for joining. I can’t wait to share more with you.

Warmly,
