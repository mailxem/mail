How to check a short link before you click it

Short links are handy. But sometimes you’d like to know where one goes before you click, especially when it arrives from someone you don’t know.

Here’s the trick: copy any Shortly link, add a “+” to the end, and paste it into your browser.

For example:

shrt.ly/garden-guide+
