Some Parrots Will Share Their Food With a Friend

By Wren Holloway
In experiments with tokens that can be swapped for food, some parrots chose to pass tokens to a neighbour, even when they got nothing back straight away. Researchers think this kind of sharing helps keep their flocks working together.

The birds were choosy: they shared more with partners they knew well. Strangers got less, which sounds familiar.
