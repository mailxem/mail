THE DEEP DIVE

I use AI every day, but not the way you’d expect

Most people’s AI workflow looks like this: open a chatbot, ask it to write something, get something generic back, rewrite it all, and wonder what the point was. That was me a year ago.

What changed was working out where each tool fits in my process:

•  Better inputs beat better prompts. What you feed the tool matters more than how you ask.
•  It’s a thinking partner, not a ghostwriter. I use it to find holes in my ideas.
•  Your voice is the thing it can’t copy. I write drafts myself and use AI to edit.
•  Automate the busywork, not the creative part.

Bottom line: AI won’t fix weak content, but it will speed you up once you know what you’re making.
