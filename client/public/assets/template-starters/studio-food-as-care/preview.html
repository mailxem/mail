KITCHEN NOTES PART 03

Food 🌿 As Care

Hello friend,

Powders. Supplements. Gummies with a vitamin for everything.

But for all the clever new things, I think the most powerful daily habit might still be dinner.

What I do trust

One of my favourite ideas in nutrition is simple: the good things in food work best together. When one ingredient is taken out of the food and put in a pill, it often doesn’t work as well.

Whole food arrives as a package: fibre, colour, and hundreds of compounds we still don’t fully understand.

We can copy one ingredient. We still can’t copy an apple.

The spice tin

I grew up with a round metal spice tin in the kitchen. The lid slid open, and inside were small bowls of cumin, turmeric, coriander, chilli, salt and pepper. A pinch here. A pinch there. Dinner begins.

Years later, I learned that some of those pairings help each other work, like black pepper with turmeric. That tin had been doing clever things all along. Rituals that taste good tend to last.

A small practice to try

This week, try one simple experiment, and if you feel like it, send me a photo :)

Make one meal the way traditional kitchens always have: beans with spices, greens with olive oil and garlic, rice with something pickled. Nothing fancy. Just food that has quietly worked for a long time.
