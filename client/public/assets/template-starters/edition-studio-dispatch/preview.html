How we wayfound four floors with two colours and one typeface.
STUDIO DISPATCH · NEW WORK

Signage for a public library that changes every year.

The brief was short: help people find the toilets, the children’s floor and the quiet room, and don’t make it look like an airport. Shelves move every year, so nothing could be printed on the walls.

We made a system of hanging panels in two colours and one typeface, which the staff can reprint and rehang themselves.
Left: colour tests under the library’s actual lights. Right: the template the staff use to make new panels.
