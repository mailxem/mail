Sam, code night

Sam started a free Tuesday coding night at the library three years ago. About twenty people come each week, most of them beginners.

How did it start?

I taught myself from library books and wanted somewhere to ask questions out loud. So I booked the meeting room and put up a poster.

What do people work on?

Personal websites, mostly. Then spreadsheets that got out of hand, and one man building a game about his allotment.

What’s the hardest part?

The library Wi-Fi. We now bring our own router.

How can people help?

If you can code, come and sit with a beginner for an hour. If you can’t, bring snacks. Both are equally useful.
