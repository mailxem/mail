Aesthetic Study · Turning the page

Last year it was fashionable to be seen reading on the train. This year, the book itself is the accessory: tote bags with bookshelves printed on them, cafés with reading-only tables, book-club dinners that sell out in minutes.

Whatever the reason, more people are carrying books. Some are even reading them.

A moment with

Ines Calder

Founder of Calder & Co.
Ines Calder makes furniture from salvaged wood and wants every piece to look like it has a past. Her third collection launches this month.

Where do your ideas come from?
Old buildings, mostly. I keep a photo of every door I like.

Three places that inspire you?
1. A timber yard by the river
2. A library reading room
3. My grandmother’s kitchen
