Two big features shipped in beta this month. Projects let you keep several sessions under one goal. And the desktop app now has a workspace where sessions, files and notes sit side by side.

Projects (beta)

Group sessions under one goal so you can hand work over without losing the thread. Each project keeps its own files, instructions and history.

Check your scripts before you ship

Tern can now run a quick evaluation on your plugins and tell you which cases pass:
