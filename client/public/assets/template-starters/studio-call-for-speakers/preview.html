Our call for speakers is open. The submission form has the details, but here are the four things you need to know:

01

We want practical talks from real experience.

Fixed a messy workflow? Run an interesting test? Built a system your team actually uses? Tell us. No product pitches, please.

02

We pay our speakers and cover the hotel.

You get a fee and travel money, and we book your hotel and most meals.

03

Never spoken on stage before? That’s fine.

We pick a mix of new and experienced speakers, and we’ll help you prepare.
