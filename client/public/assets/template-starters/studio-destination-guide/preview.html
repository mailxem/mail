Port Alma

Port Alma is a city where the old town walls meet the sea, painted houses lean over narrow streets, and every meal seems to involve fish caught that morning.

Off the main square, things get quieter: courtyards, small museums and cafés where nobody is in a hurry.

How to see the old town without the crowds

Go early. By 9am the cruise groups arrive; before that the squares are yours.

•  Walk the city walls at sunrise
•  Have breakfast in the market
•  Save the museums for the afternoon heat

Tip: the best views are from the fort on the hill, a 20-minute walk from the centre.
