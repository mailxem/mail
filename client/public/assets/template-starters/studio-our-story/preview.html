Thank you for joining our crew! When our son was born with a condition that affects how he moves, everything changed for our family.

As he grew, we wanted to make things better for him. The clothes made for kids like him were either uncomfortable or impossible to find. Zips in the wrong places, fabrics that rubbed, nothing that worked with a wheelchair. Not good.

If you’ve found us, you’ve probably been dealing with this too.

With help from other families, we designed a range that fits real life. Our adaptive basics are a good place to start, and our sample pack helps you find the right size before buying more.
