THE CARE GUIDE

Look after it and it will see you through a lot of winters.

1. Brush it. A soft clothes brush, top to bottom, after a few wears. It lifts dust before it settles in.
2. Air it. Hang it by a window overnight instead of washing it. Wool sheds most smells on its own.
3. Spot clean. Blot spills with cold water. Don’t rub, and never put it in the dryer.
4. Hang it properly. A wide wooden hanger keeps the shoulders in shape. Store it in a cotton bag over summer.
