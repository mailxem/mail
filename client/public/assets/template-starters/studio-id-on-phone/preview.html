DIGITAL ID PILOT

Show your ID.
With a tap.

Now that you’ve added your ID to your wallet as part of the pilot, you can show it at selected checkpoints and businesses where proof of age is needed, with a tap of your phone or watch. You never need to hand your device over.
Learn how to show your ID ›

Your details. Private and secure.

Your ID is encrypted and protected against tampering and theft. No record of where you show it is kept, and your face or fingerprint keeps it safe.

Digital ID is spreading.

You can show your ID at selected checkpoints, shops and for age-restricted deliveries. Keep carrying your physical ID for now, but expect more places to accept it soon.
Learn more about digital ID ›

Manage your ID from your device.

Renew, update your address and more on the issuing office’s website. Changes appear on your digital ID automatically.
