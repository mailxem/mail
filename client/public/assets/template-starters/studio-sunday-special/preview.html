COVER STORY

Shoppers are starting the holidays early this year

Retailers say people began their holiday shopping weeks earlier than usual, spreading spending out to cope with higher prices.

•  Early deals made up a bigger share of sales than last year
•  Online orders grew faster than in-store visits

Why it matters: an early start can mean a quieter rush in December, or just a longer one.
MONEY

Gold is having its best year in decades

Investors nervous about the economy have pushed gold to record prices. Jewellers say they’re seeing more people sell old pieces than buy new ones.
HISTORY

A museum robbery, solved by a library card

Police say a thief who took a set of antique coins was caught after leaving a borrowed book at the scene. The library confirmed it was overdue.
ON SCREEN

A cancelled show is back, by popular demand

Fans ran a two-year campaign to bring back a cult comedy. This week, the first new episode drew its biggest audience yet.
FOOD

Everyone’s talking about a soup

A simple bean soup from a small café went viral after a customer posted the recipe card. The café now sells the card for $1, with proceeds going to a food bank.
