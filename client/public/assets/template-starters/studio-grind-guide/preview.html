OUR TOP TIPS

For a better cup at home

Choose your brewer, then your grind.
The brewer decides the grind. Espresso wants fine. Filter wants medium. A cafetière wants coarse.
Grind just before you brew.
Ground coffee goes stale in minutes. If you buy it ground, keep it airtight, cool and dark.
Taste, then adjust.
Watery or sour? Go finer. Harsh or bitter? Go coarser.
