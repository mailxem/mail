Hi there,

We take getting things done seriously, so we’re happy to say this was our busiest year yet. We shipped 120 improvements to make Jotter faster, more reliable and easier to use.

In the summer we shared our mid-year update. Here are a few highlights since then:
•  Write faster with more shortcuts and a better insert menu.
•  Stay focused with focus mode and quick calculations.
•  Find things faster with clearer search results.
•  A smoother app with multi-note selection on mobile.
And we’re not stopping: our next big update is coming early next year, with three new assistant features: summaries, smarter search and meeting notes.

In our monthly recap video, our product lead shows the new features and explains what it’s taken to get them ready.
