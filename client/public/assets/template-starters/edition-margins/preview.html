MARGINS — ESSAY NO. 9

On leaving things unfinished

There is a drawer in my studio for projects I have stopped working on. I used to call it the failure drawer. Now I call it the drawer.

Some of what is in there will never come out. A few things have come back years later, when I finally knew what they were for. A poster from 2019 became the cover of a book last spring.

“Unfinished is a state, not a verdict.”

So this month I am not finishing anything. I’m opening the drawer and writing down what each thing was trying to be. If you have a drawer like that, reply and tell me what’s in it.

— J.
