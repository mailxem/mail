When we joined forces, the goal was simple: close the gap between finding inspiration and making something.

Since then you’ve had more chances to learn and connect, and your feedback has shaped what we built. Thank you ♥

But going from inspiration to building still meant two separate trips.

So we changed that.

Brushwork is now
Good Mail Studio.

Everything you’ve made in Brushwork now lives inside Good Mail Studio. You can move between finding inspiration and building emails in one place.

This is just the start. Some things, like logos and colours, are still changing, so you may spot a bit of the old name here and there.

What this means for you:

01.

Your account, designs, folders and team stay exactly as they are.

02.

You can sign in at the new address or the old one. We’ll send you to the right place.

03.

“Get inspired” in the sidebar takes you straight to the gallery.

04.

You can now save emails from the gallery and come back to them later.

05.

If you had both accounts with the same email address, they’re now linked.

06.

You’ll start getting our newsletter twice a week. Change your email preferences any time.
