Nine kilometres, one steep climb, and the best view in the valley.
TRAIL REPORT
ROUTE 12 · THE RIDGE LOOP
DISTANCE

9 km

CLIMB

420 m

TIME

3½ hrs

Start at the church car park.

Follow the stream for the first two kilometres, then take the stile on the left. The climb is steady for forty minutes and steep for the last ten. At the top, keep the wall on your right all the way along the ridge.

Come down through the beech wood. It’s muddy after rain, so leave the trainers at home.
BEFORE YOU GO
No phone signal on the ridge. Download the map, and tell someone your route.
