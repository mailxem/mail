Learning to slow down

Meditation isn’t about becoming a different person. It’s practice at noticing what’s going on, and getting a bit of distance from it. This two-minute video explains how.

When should I practise?

First thing in the morning works for most people: it’s quiet, and it’s easy to make a habit of. Any time is fine, though, as long as it’s roughly the same time each day.

Where should I practise?

No need to climb a mountain. Anywhere reasonably quiet where you won’t be interrupted for ten minutes is just right.
