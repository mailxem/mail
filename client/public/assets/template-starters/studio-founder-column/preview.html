My Late-Night Moment

A late-night comedy show used a clip of me this week. Be careful what you wish for!

The segment was about subscriptions, and they dug up an old interview where I talked about fridges that reorder milk by themselves. The host called it “mildly terrifying.” Fair.

Honestly? I’m a fan, so being teased on TV felt like an honour. It was a good rant: printers that stop working when a trial ends, cancel buttons hidden three menus deep, monthly fees for heated seats.

Jokes aside, it was strange to see an idea this community spent years working on turn up in a comedy monologue. We went from a small billing company to a topic people argue about at dinner. That doesn’t happen often.
