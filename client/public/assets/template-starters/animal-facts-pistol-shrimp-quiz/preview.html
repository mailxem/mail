FACT OF THE DAY / ACROSS SPECIES

A tiny shrimp creates one of the ocean’s loudest sounds.

By the Animal Facts editors
Pistol shrimp are small, but their oversized snapping claw creates a powerful jet of water. The jet forms a bubble that collapses with a sharp sound—a remarkable example of the physics hiding in the natural world.
The snap helps the shrimp hunt and communicate. Follow the story to learn how researchers study this tiny animal and the surprisingly noisy world beneath the surface.
