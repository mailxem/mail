# Xem journal: launch research

Research checked September 10, 2026. This is a qualitative topic and search-intent plan, not a keyword-volume study. Real Chrome was used to visit the sources. Google, DuckDuckGo, and Brave returned anti-bot challenges; Bing returned a shell without usable results, and Yahoo search failed. No challenges were bypassed. Search Console, Ads Keyword Planner, and paid keyword datasets were not available. Accordingly, there are no invented volume, difficulty, ranking, or “fastest growing” claims.

## Why these topics

Current authoritative material supports a useful cluster around AI assistance, permission, measurement quality, and sender requirements. The Litmus accessibility guide is dated March 24, 2026; ICO direct marketing guidance was updated April 28, 2026. Gmail, Yahoo, and Outlook requirements were checked directly. Mailchimp's AI resource establishes terminology and practical use cases, not measured 2026 demand. HubSpot's email-trends article showed an October 2024 update despite a 2026 report banner; it was not presented as new research.

## Editorial policy

Ten original articles, approximately 870–983 words each. No invented personal experience, authors, statistics, or case studies. Example data is identified as illustrative. Source links appear in every article. Organization byline and visible AI-assistance disclosure; no false claim of human authorship. General testing/automation techniques are distinguished from verified current product functionality.
