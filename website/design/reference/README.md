# Reference study: Wispr Flow

Reference: https://wisprflow.ai/
Browsed in real Google Chrome using Playwright on 2026-09-10.

## Observed

- A centered floating navigation bar stays present while scrolling. Its warm cream surface and visible border preserve contrast across the green and photographic sections.
- EB Garamond headings combine roman and italic phrases. Oversized type, compact body text, and generous whitespace carry the layout.
- The hero animates a ribbon of transcription through a central voice control. This visual demonstrates transformation rather than serving as an unrelated background.
- Full-width, rounded transitions move from cream to near-black to deep green. Sticky product stories pair changing visual states with short explanations.
- The FAQ uses a question list beside an answer panel on desktop. Clicking “Does Flow work in other languages?” changes the answer without navigation.
- The mobile hamburger opens a full navigation surface; it closes on a second activation.
- A large closing call to action repeats the headline’s visual language.

## Xem translation

The website borrows pacing, editorial type, restrained colors, persistent navigation, and product-led motion. Its original illustration turns a note into an envelope. A lightweight 3D ribbon connects the email designs. Real Xem screens replace the reference’s video demonstrations. The customer journey, template gallery, and content are specific to Xem.
